**Habib, Petr & Rust (Stanford Neurosurgery/Stroke Center; Tracked Biotechnologies; USC Zilkha) — bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.10.26.762043v1, posted 27 Oct 2026, "Focal neuronal loss and cell replacement in a connectome model of fly steering"** | FULL READ (abstract → results → discussion → methods). Stroke/TBI-motivated: which cells and connections must be RESTORED to recover a behaviour. Substrate = **MaleCNS v1.0 — our own second graph** — 165,122 traced neurons / 25,563,197 directed connections / 124,025,046 synapses, run as the published Shiu-style LIF with UNTUNED parameters (dv/dt = (−v+g)/20 ms, dg/dt = −g/5 ms, 7 mV threshold, 2.2 ms refractory, DT 0.1 ms, 1.8 ms synaptic delay; GABA/glutamate/histamine inhibitory, everything else excitatory; per-synapse 0.14390733 mV = the source model's 0.275 mV scaled by the ratio of average synapses per neuron, "without fitting lesion outcomes"). Readout = 32 wing-steering motor neurons decoded by a FIXED antisymmetrised right-minus-left weight vector calibrated once on 5 intact seeds, giving gains/bias/motor-drive into a 2-D steering controller run 10 s against matched wind gusts; the network is measured ONCE per realization (300 ms trial, 50–300 ms window) and the controller then runs on those fixed gains — no visual feedback, no flight biomechanics, stated as such. | **The numbers that matter.** Lesion screen: 56 fixed spatial centres × nearest {1,000 / 4,000 / 16,000} eligible somata (all 815 annotated motor neurons PROTECTED as a common output population) — median straightness .905 → .880 → .345, and at 4,000 lost cells mean straightness ranged **.042 to .952 across sites** (location, not size, picks the deficit). Nested site-20 series: distance **84.0% of intact at 250 lost → 6.5% at 2,000 → 0.58% at 64,000**, with a PRE-SPECIFIED spared-response control (left tangential cell LPTC_L_L) holding **101.4% at 32,000 and 91.0% at 64,000**. **The count-matched removal contrast — the cell closest to our own claim:** inside the 1,000-cell site-20 mask, removing all **37 descending neurons → 57.4%** of intact distance vs removing **37 OTHER cells sampled from the SAME mask → 101.0%** (and the 4 lobula-plate tangential cells alone → 98.0%). Replacement after the 2,000-cell lesion, 100 cells restored with their original connections: motor-control priority **62.5% [60.9–64.0]** vs **random-100 10.1% [9.1–11.1]** vs **random-1,000 48.5%** vs projection priority 20.3% (the chosen 100 = 79 DNs + 5 LPTCs + 16 other visual = every DN the lesion took). **The identity axis:** at the SAME 100 intended positions under one type-based integration rule, matched identities **60.0% [58.3–61.6]** vs non-target central-brain identities **6.7% [6.3–7.0]**; at 2,000 replacements **87.6% vs 9.3%**; random nearby wiring 8.7% [8.0–9.4] at 100 and 14.3% [12.7–16.0] at 2,000; complete original reconstruction reproduces intact output exactly (their implementation check). Scale: 79 conditions, 7,900 neural×intervention realizations (neural seeds 1000–1099, intervention seeds 2000–2099, paired by realization), 158,000 trajectories, **independent unit = the realization, not its 20 gust trajectories**. Their own disclosures: 89 of the 100 matched-identity cells copied partners from a reflected opposite-side neuron of the same type, "so for most cells this rule approximates the original wiring through bilateral symmetry"; a **15% synaptic-amplitude reduction abolished intact wing-motor output**, so "the collapse of wing-motor output after larger lesions may therefore partly reflect this proximity to threshold"; and behaviour improved (62.5%) while wing-motor output sat at 40.9% and the leftward steering imbalance went uncorrected — "a more normal trajectory therefore did not establish restoration of the original circuit". | **What it changes for peabrain.** (a) **Our novelty claim narrows for the second time in two ticks, and this is now the closest prior art to the identity half specifically.** ApproxBrain (t80, folded L174a) established single-cell necessity inside a curated, searched subset; this paper establishes that **identity beats number** in a connectome-constrained fly model with explicit count-matched controls.
