**Figure 5 — three readouts per arm: the honesty panel.** Right-tibia mean duty for all 37 arms of the B39 scan (banc/l184/threepanel.csv; identities from banc/l175/results_l175.json), one row per arm with three dots: duty under the chopped 5-s-on/4-s-off 0.7-Hz gate (green; mean over 16 matched seeds), duty in the continuous 1.4-Hz middle segment (blue; same 16 seeds), and cross-seed duty — flex and extensor traces from different seed pairs under the chopped gate (yellow; mean over the 240 ordered, nonindependent pairs). Duty is the fraction of 500-sample windows with r(flex, ext) ≤ −0.25; the x-axis is that fraction on a plain linear 0–0.8 scale (no transform), and rows are sorted by chopped duty, high to low, so the ordering emphasizes the chopped readout rather than ranking the other two. Small k-columns give the count of runs (of 16) or pairs (of 240) with duty ≥ 0.5; no values were missing, so no markers were omitted. Highlighted rows: the carrier DNp33-L (sc25, green label), its mass and drive twins (sc26, dmA, dmC, purple), the two boundary cells (sc27, sc40 — sc40 shares the carrier's leading relay GI 75494 — blue), and the k=24 cut base (cutwA, orange). Reading: the carrier arm is the only one high on all three readouts (0.758 / 0.510 / 0.622), so its phenotype is neither a seed artifact nor gated by the chopping alone; the drive twins instead split — low chopped duty (dmA 0.390, dmC 0.366) but high cross-seed duty (0.376, 0.287) — the signature of timing shared across seeds rather than per-run gating (L184 Q3), while nearly every other arm collapses toward zero in the 1.4-Hz column (exceptions sc47 0.310, dmC 0.192, sc33 0.109) and hugs the base band (cutwA 0.408) under the gate.
