### 2.4 Mechanism: the carrier's channel, isolated at an unchanged gain
One activity arm isolates the carrier; the edge ladder then locates its
channel. Per-arm levels and seed counts live in the grid table below, so the
prose carries only the paired legs, pools and statics the table cannot hold.

**The clean arm (silW): the flip without a gain change, without an edge
touched.** On the cutwA PARTIAL base (cohort ranks 1–24 cut), clamping ONLY
DNp33-L's activity — no edge removed, no cell deleted — flips the regime to
SUPER: .622 with 14/16 seeds on B30 [l144.silw] and .659 with
14/16 on B33 [l150.h1], paired ΔR against the base +.189
(6/16 seeds up and 0 down) and +.281 (9/16 up and 0 down),
canonical pooled 78 of 98, Wilson
[.706,.864], against the untreated cut base +.517
[+.410,+.602], Fisher p = 7e-19 [l172.pooled, l172.diffs]. Its gain is
BIT-IDENTICAL to its own base's — g = .143658196 in both batches, exact float
equality in the two stored jsons [l173b.gains] — so the flip is no artefact of
the active-fraction re-bisect, and it is the lineage's only flip arm with that
property. Every edge-removal flip arm carries a small af-matched gain INCREASE
(eOutInt +1.297%, cutng25 and eAll +1.244%, eOutAll +1.238%, eIn +1.087%,
eMotIn +1.062%), the two non-flip arms sit within ±0.03% (eMotTop5 −0.031%,
eMotBot5 +0.009%), and eOutMot sits at −0.568% [l173b.gains]. Identity and
gain therefore CO-VARY across the edge ladder, a confound untested until this
pass: silW breaks it from the activity side, §2.7's graph × gain 2×2 from the
wiring side [l169.grid, l169d.grid].

**The same clamp is substrate-gated.** On the INTACT closure the identical
clamp does not move the readout detectably: paired ΔR −.088 against three
random-cell clamp shams at .429/.502/.504 — n = 3 shams is a spread of three
numbers, not an interval, and |−.088|
is LARGER than their .075 range, so this arm is reported as "no demonstrated
effect", never as "inside the sham band" [l137.g1, l137.g3] (replicated
−.036 on B30 [l144.silf]); a tonic VOLLEY moves +.022 against the sham's
+.077 [l137.g2]. The clamp is live on both substrates (dm .0486 vs 0.0 for
bit-identical dead arms) and the carrier is active in the operating regime
(census x_mean .454 [l137.g1]), so the intact subnet ABSORBS it rather than
lacking the channel. Its firing is load-bearing exactly when the cohort's
drive is gone: the intact cohort makes it redundant; on the cut base its
silence removes the last pro-structure input. (This amends the v2 "activity
carries nothing" reading: same cell, same machinery, inverted verdict.)

**Cohort drive is activity-mediated too, with a disclosed limit.** Clamping
the whole rank-1–24 cohort's ACTIVITY with its wiring intact lands in the same
class as the k=24 CUT, with the Ti_L death .119 (0/16) [l137.sil24]; the label
matches the cut base (.383, 5/16), but on that batch the
untreated intact base is
6/16, so sil24 is a class-label match with NO demonstrated separation from
either comparator at n = 16.

**Why the carrier is redundant on the intact base is not input overlap**
(static decomposition, observation, [l151.sheet]): it is already the
dominant single DN input into its 70-cell intrinsic sheet, the other
57 cohort cells combined delivering 0.33× its mass (DN-sheet input share
14.5% intact vs 14.7% cut), and its motor lever is cut-invariant — one
left-side intermediary (IN06B033) carries its two-hop pathway into all
five right-side antagonist pools at an unchanged DN-input mix, while the left
flexor pool is unreachable through the sheet (two-hop share 0.100 into Ti_fR,
no pathway at all into Ti_fL [l151.crossed]): the static mirror of the dynamic
crossed law. The substrate gate lives in the dynamics — which regime routes
through the channel — not in a static redundancy differential [l151.verdict].

**Where the channel lives: the edge decomposition** (edge cuts on the cutwA
PARTIAL base, fresh B29, n=16/arm, paired-noise fork). All 80 of the carrier's
out-edges live in the window — 70 onto the vnc_intrinsic sheet (.005599
removal mass), 10 onto motor pools (.000837) — plus 13 in-edges, all from
other DNs. Cutting ONLY the 70 intrinsic out-edges completes the flip (ΔR vs
base +.404 [l142.h2a]) at .0056 removed mass,
9.72× below the .0544 any-geometry scattered band: identity beats mass at cell
scale. Cutting ONLY the 13 in-edges carries it independently (ΔR +.417
[l142.h3]) — two redundant channels, one per side of the cell — while the 10
motor out-edges alone land between classes [l142.h2b].

The motor sheet closes the dose question as a THRESHOLD FOOTPRINT: neither
5-edge half moves anything on either batch (heavy +.007, light −.013, both
inside the .075 clamp/noise floor, per-seed order sign count 8/16 = coin flip
[l145.dose]; canonical pooled top5 31 of 98 [.233,.414] and bot5
25 of 98 [.179,.350], neither distinguishable from the base), while all ten
together SUPER [l145.mot] at pooled 21 of 34 [.450,.761] [l172.pooled],
strictly between base and carrier [l172.diffs]: a re-draw RATE between
endpoints, not graded mass — the §2.5 endpoints-vs-interior law recurring
inside one cell's footprint. In-edge redundancy holds on the fresh batch
(motor+in combo [l145.motin]).

Two further grid cells are bitwise identities that hold BY CONSTRUCTION and
are reported in Methods §3.8 as sanity checks rather than as findings here
[l142.aeall, l148.seid]. What they leave as a RESULT is the margin: the
~.13 gap from silW (.622 on B30 [l144.silw]) to eAll (pooled .757
[l148.pooled.eAll_B29_B32]) is settle-history carried by the edges, never
window activity — activity silence is a strict subset of edge removal. eAll
SUPER replicates on its second batch (.768, 15/16 [l148.eall]).

**Reading (interpretation, labeled):** the carrier's causal footprint is its
EDGE SET, and its firing matters only where the cohort's drive no longer
covers for it — one channel, reachable from either side of the cell, gated by
which regime routes through it. That also explains the gain-ensemble split of
§2.5: the carrier endpoint is decided by the PRESENCE of a fixed 93-edge set
(jitter moves weights, not membership), while the transition interior is a
mass balance near threshold that the ±8% af-matched re-gains do move.
