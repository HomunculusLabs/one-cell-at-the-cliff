## 1. Introduction

We report four load-bearing results, and state them up front:
(1) at gain-certified MATCHED operating points, cohort removal collapses
right-side antagonist structure then super-raises it — kill 5 of 178 at k=19,
super 188 of 194 at k=29 [l172.pooled], a non-monotone window rather than a
dose curve. (2) The transition step is carried by ONE cell's IDENTITY, not its
removal mass (mass-twin XOR: the twin lands INTERMEDIATE at
30 of 66, [.340,.574] — above the base by +.175 [+.047,+.305] and below the
carrier by −.512 [−.628,−.388] [l172.pooled, l172.diffs]). That carrier is
SUFFICIENT, not unique, and set-dependently so: of seven carrier-free cohorts
mass-matched to the same window, one reaches super (39/48 [l168.scr]) and six
do not. The carrier's causal footprint is its EDGE SET, and its own firing
matters only where the cohort's drive no longer covers for it (§2.4). (3) The
carrier's own ACTIVITY is substrate-gated: not detectably moving on the intact
subnet, flip-completing on the cut base (pooled 78 of 98, Wilson [.706,.864]
[l172.pooled]). (4) Frequency claims are BAND-RELATIVE, and the sub-band line
that prompted the rule is STIMULUS-SET: the drive envelope's own spectrum
peaks at .100 Hz [l171.envelope], exactly where the measured line sits. The
identity contrast does not ride on it — re-scored in the unchopped fast
segment (12/16 vs 0/16) and after a 0.3-Hz high-pass (15/16 vs 1/16), the
carrier-vs-twin ordering is preserved [l171.panel]. The slow-line censuses
themselves (sub-band in 449 of 449 baseline traces on unique seeds
[l172.census_unique]; a Tr/Ta/Sr pool-subset carrier, 8 of 20 [l157.pooled])
move to §2.8 and §4 as limitations.

Connectome-constrained models now reproduce activity or behaviour in several
systems — the fly visual lineage [1], the whole-brain sensorimotor model [2],
an untrained brain-and-nerve-cord simulation [3], a frozen larval rate
operator [7], and connectome-constrained recurrent fits whose predicted
activity is compared against measurement [30]. Duckophila [35] is an
independent whole-brain leaky-integrate-and-fire duck on FlyWire v783 [10]
driving a MuJoCo body; its degree-preserving rewiring collapses startle to 0
of 20 animals vs intact 1.00 [.91,1.00] with an input-null at 0.50 honestly
disclosing the encoder's share. That null-control form is the one our §2
mirrors at rate fidelity. The open question has moved from DOES wiring matter
to WHICH wiring decisions matter — at what granularity (statistics vs exact
placement vs individual cells).

What a perturbation in a model can and cannot license is itself an old
question, and we inherit its instruments rather than re-invent them.
Degenerate parameter sets produce the same network output [29], and lesioning
a system whose ground truth is known exposes how little a deficit alone
licenses [26]. Contribution attribution handles interaction terms explicitly
[27, 28]. From the bench side, acute manipulations carry off-target effects
[24, 25], and even a clean genetic silencer acts on a circuit rather than on a
function [23]. Network-control work on Caenorhabditis elegans predicted neuron
function from wiring and tested single-cell ablations in the animal [31], and
a signal-propagation atlas then measured how far wiring predicts functional
connectivity [32]. Our twin control is the mass-matched member of that family.

Walking-CPG field context (Sapkal et al. [34]) supplies three findings that
bear on this one: per-leg CPG modules survive near-total deafferentation —
substrate removal symmetricizes step-cycles (duty → 0.5) while joint temporal
ORDER is conserved. FeCO proprioceptive input modulates frequency and
strengthens coupling but is never necessary. And their wet-lab motif's
IN12B003 strongly drives tibia-FLEXOR motor neurons that the modeling triad
left non-rhythmic — field support for flexor-first pools as rhythmogenic
substrate, the same pools our transition gates. AMENDMENT (their signed
direction, tested): we preregistered deafferentation-speeds-the-CPG as a
prediction and ran it at rate fidelity (whole-run mech-OFF, fresh batch B34,
paired seeds): NOT MET — dominant-frequency deltas go 6+/10− of 16, p=.4545
[l154.freq]. Removal re-draws the attractor per seed (anchor seeds
.70/.32 Hz on → .45/1.09 off) rather than speeding the rhythm — the sensory
sinusoid was the ENTRAINER, not a throttle. The STRUCTURE axis matches the
field: antagonist antiphase S07 survives deafferentation on both live sides
(Ti L +.61 / R +.72, Tr R +.76) and the dead left-Tr channel wakes
(−.08 → +.04) [l154.structure] — alternation needs no sensory drive, the
CPG-central claim.

Nearest neighbours and the gap come from the targeted novelty sweep logged in
LANE-RESEARCH.md 2026-11-18. There are four neighbours and one gap.

(a) ApproxBrain [6] is the closest formal neighbour, and closer than an
earlier draft of this paper allowed. It certifies cost-indexed subset
SUFFICIENCY on the hand-curated 148-neuron walking circuit of [4], and it does
report that the circuit's three interneurons are individually NECESSARY — not
partner-substitutable — with a mean task distance of .017
over 32 held-out parameter draws and no failing replicate. Single-cell
necessity in a connectome-constrained model is therefore established prior
art, and our contrast narrows to two things it does not do: the subset is
curated and searched rather than emergent in a bulk-extracted subnet, and
necessity is compared against other subsets rather than against a cell of
matched removal mass.

(b) The frozen larval rate operator [7] shows exact placement, rather than
degree and weight statistics, governs specific response modes — a whole-graph
claim, not a single-cell causal carrier.

(c) Wet-lab single-neuron work anchors single-cell SUFFICIENCY in the animal:
descending pathway anatomy and optogenetic activation screens [14, 18], the
giant-fibre escape decision [33], a dedicated brain circuit for forward
walking [13], and the premotor organisation that those commands act through
[15, 16, 17]. In-model,
FlyHack [5] stress-tests a connectome-constrained visual model and finds
mechanistic conclusions swing with the comparison model, the intervention form
and the trained solution — greedy cell-type sets meet its tuning criterion in
only 25 of 50 ensemble members, and one attribution score falls from .872 to
.126 when the same ground truth is scored against a different intervention
form. That is the robustness critique our gain ensemble (§2.5) and batch
pooling (§2.9) answer directly.

(d) Focal neuronal loss and cell replacement in a connectome model of fly
steering [36] is the closest prior art to the identity half of this paper. On
the male central nervous system connectome [9], run as an untuned spiking
model of the kind in [2], removing all 37 descending neurons inside a
1,000-cell spatial mask leaves 57.4% of intact steering distance, while 37
other cells drawn from the same mask leave 101.0%. After a 2,000-cell lesion,
restoring 100 cells by motor-control priority recovers 62.5% [60.9-64.0] of
intact displacement against 10.1% [9.1-11.1] for a random hundred. At the same
100 intended positions, matched identities reach 60.0% [58.3-61.6] against
6.7% [6.3-7.0] for non-target central-brain identities. Identity beating cell
number in a connectome-constrained fly model is therefore prior art as well,
and we scope their result as they do:
89 of their 100 matched cells copy their partners from a reflected neuron of
the same type, so the finding is strongest as
type-dependent integration rather than as single-cell identity. Our contrast
with it is four specific things, and "identity matters" is not one of them.
First, matching currency: their controls are
matched on cell count and ours on removal mass. Count-matching is the weaker
control, because 37 cells drawn from a spatial mask carry only a fraction of
37 descending neurons' out-mass, and our carrier sits
9.72× below any mass-only band (§2.2). Second, resolution:
their unit is a class — 37 descending neurons, 100-cell grafts, a cell type —
while ours is one cell against one mass twin. Third, readout: theirs is a
behavioural scalar decoded through a hand-built open-loop controller whose
network is measured once per realization, ours an emergent structure statistic
inside the network's own dynamics. Fourth, direction and substrate:
they remove and then add cells on the male central nervous system, we remove
on the female cord.

The gap this paper fills, stated after both narrowings above:
not that identity matters — [6] and [36] show that already — but that an
all-or-none identity step EMERGES in an UNCURATED bulk-extracted subnet and
survives a MASS-matched twin control, with the carrier's own removal mass
9.72× below any band where mass alone reproduces the step. The supporting
claims are the preregistered twin and scramble controls, replication across
fresh seed batches with pooled intervals, and a gain ensemble that bounds
operating-point proximity.

## 2. Results

### 2.1 The dose cliff (Fig 1 = peabrain-figs/l136_transition_law.png)

The substrate is the l65 cord subnet, 20,521 cord + 728 DN-into cells, and the
Ti flexor pool has n=101 [subnet]; 11 runs are summed [n_runs_summed]. The
U-law is read with pooled Wilson intervals, and the k=24→25 step (+.371 meanR
[xor.steps]) is the transition. The ladder is non-monotone: k=58 kills again
(1 of 258 [l172.pooled]) — a WINDOW, not a monotone dose response. Its LEFT
side is the opposite story (253 of 258), so k=58 kills the right pair, not the
cord. Every arm's count in this paper is its single canonical pooled count
(§2.9): the unique-seed union over all admissible batches.

### 2.2 Identity, not mass: the twin XOR

At the transition, rank 25 = id 1110 (DNp33-L — THE carrier; m .00644), rank
26 = id 319 (DNpe025-R, the twin; m .00604), delta 3.9e-04 [xor]. 1110-alone ⇒
SUPER (203 of 210 pooled, [.933,.984]); 319-alone ⇒ INTERMEDIATE
(30 of 66, [.340,.574]) [l172.pooled]. The twin is not neutral and the
question is not underpowered at the canonical pool: twin − base = +.175, 95%
CI [+.047,+.305], Fisher p = .008, while twin − carrier = −.512 [−.628,−.388]
[l172.diffs]. On the smaller two-batch pool the same contrast was
indistinguishable from zero, so the earlier "neutral" label was a pooling
artefact, not a measured null.

Both READS of the flip ride the same cell (right-side super-raise AND
left-side death) — crossed effect. The canonical pooled counts (unique seeds,
all admissible batches) are: carrier cell 203 of 210, Wilson [.933,.984], mean
duty .766 with bootstrap [.749,.783]; cutwA base 72 of 258, [.228,.337], mean
duty .404 [.385,.425] [l172.pooled]. Carrier − base = +.688 [+.621,+.742]
[l172.diffs].

Three things follow, and they re-scope the headline claim:

1. **Not unique.** One carrier-free mass-matched cohort — the heaviest,
   cutscr0 — does reach the super class on the right (39/48, lb .681, three
   batches). So removing DNp33-L is a SUFFICIENT route to the transition at
   this window, not the only one. The manuscript's claim is accordingly
   sufficiency-under-mass-control, never uniqueness.
2. **Set-dependent, not mass-dependent.** The seven scramble sets span only
   .0439–.0544 of extra removed mass yet span the entire outcome range. The
   SECOND-heaviest set kills outright (cutscr1, .0476, 0/32) while two
   LIGHTER sets sit well above it (cutscr3 .0442 at 17/32; cutscrL1 .0460 at
   10/16), so the right-side outcome is not monotone — not even ordered — in
   removed mass among carrier-free sets. That is the same conclusion the
   two-cell XOR reaches one level down.
3. **Scrambles do not reproduce the crossed state.** cutscr0 raises BOTH
   sides above the untreated base band (right 39/48 vs 16/48; left 35/48 vs
   15/48) [l168.base]. The carrier's signature is CROSSED — right-side
   super-raise WITH left-side death (§2.2 above). So the one scramble that
   matches the carrier on the claim axis still differs from it on the other
   side: same class, different state.

### 2.3 The carrier is not statically predictable

The carrier cell DNp33-L is ACh, neck-connective class; 80 out-edges, 87%
intrinsic / 13% motor, ZERO direct antagonist-pool edges, mid-pack
removal-mass rank (34/58); the mass-twin carries the family's only
interconnect edges — the carrier is an input-side isolate. (Sources:
l134/static.json, l137 static tables; label as observation, not mechanism
claim.)
